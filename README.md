# cheungming_chengleever
I'm Cheung-Ming (Ming) Cheng-Leever, a JD/MBA candidate at the University of Hawaiʻi at Mānoa and a legal secretary at Porter Kiakona Kopper, LLC, a Honolulu firm that works with condominium associations. Behind every letter I help prepare, there's a community of neighbors trying to make shared living work, and that's what makes the job meaningful. I'm especially drawn to real property law, where legal questions shape people's homes and financial futures. As a Chinese American, I'm also fascinated by China–U.S. relations and their impact across the Pacific. I earned my BBA in Finance and International Business from the Shidler College of Business. At the firm, I've learned how much legal work depends on care: getting the details right, meeting every deadline, and treating each file as if it matters to someone, because it does. Within eight years, I hope to become a partner at a law firm, and to be an attorney clients trust with what they value most. My next step is finishing my dual JD/MBA. Outside work and school, I've trained in karate for over 19 years and now teach it, which has given me discipline and patience. I also love cooking, playing bass guitar, and yoga.
